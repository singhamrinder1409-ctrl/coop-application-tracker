# AI And Sources Note

## AI Use

Generative AI was used to help draft the project files, SQL schema, ERD notes, normalization notes, and video outline. The group reviewed the content and should make sure the final version matches what we actually understand and can explain.

We are using AI as a helper for drafting and organizing the submission, not as a replacement for understanding the database design. In the video, we should be ready to explain the tables, keys, foreign keys, and normalization choices ourselves.

## Course Materials Used

The design was based on the CSC 370 project instructions and lecture slides provided by the course. The relevant ideas include:

- SQL DDL and `CREATE TABLE`
- relational databases as tables with tuples and attributes
- conceptual design and ERDs
- entity sets, attributes, identifiers, and relationships
- relationship multiplicity
- functional dependencies
- keys and superkeys
- BCNF and decomposition
- foreign keys and referential integrity
- constraints in relational algebra
- inheritance and weak entity sets for future design improvements
